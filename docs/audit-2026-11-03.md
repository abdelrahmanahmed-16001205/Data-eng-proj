# Website audit — 3 November 2026

Scope: `website/` against the Milestone 1 brief (`Project Information/Project Description.pdf`).
Deadline: 9 November 2026.

**Verdict: 74/100 — launchable with caveats.** Craft baseline is high; the gaps are deployment
freshness, an under-explained method, and missing contribution attribution.

---

## 1. Already strong

- Structure matches the brief exactly: four dropdowns (borough / completion year / construction
  type / meter status), search mode, central **Generate report**, twelve charts Q01–Q12, and a
  story section covering question → findings → limits → impact.
- Search handles the brief's own example. Typing `Brooklyn 2022 new buildings` and pressing Enter
  sets borough=BROOKLYN, year=2022, construction=New Construction, and reports 197 housing records
  and 4,254 meter records.
- Accessibility is above the class norm. axe-core 4.10.2 reports **0 violations** on the filtered
  desktop state and at 390px. Mobile charts do not shrink to unreadable type: `.chart svg` locks to
  620px below 640px and scrolls horizontally with `tabindex` and a `role="group"` label.
- Impeccable detector: **0 findings** at 1440, 1024, 768, 390 and 320 after the 3 November fixes
  (17 before: 5 `line-length`, 12 `nested-cards`, plus 4 advisory `straggler` notes).

## 5. Accessibility — beyond compliance

axe reports 0 violations but only checks what it can compute (149 `incomplete` colour-contrast nodes
are SVG text axe cannot resolve; the 22 documented contrast pairs were verified manually).

- **SVG `aria-label` is a run-on series.** Q04's label describes 2,540 points. Replace with a short
  summary plus a `<table>` in `<details>`.
- **Values are hover-only.** `<title>` on shapes serves a mouse, not a keyboard or a touch screen.
- **`aria-live` announces record counts but not that twelve charts were replaced.** Add
  "12 charts updated".
- **Filters can go stale silently.** Changing a dropdown does nothing until Generate, with no signal.

## 6. Performance — measured

- **Payload:** 5.72 MB raw across two CSVs → **1.19 MB gzipped** (buildings 6.0×, meters 4.0×).
  `python -m http.server` sends the raw 5.72 MB, which is why localhost feels slow; GitHub Pages
  compresses.
- **DOM:** **13,312 nodes** after full render. The map alone is **5,100** elements; three scatter
  charts are ~2,540 each. Render after Generate is **53 ms** on desktop. A Queens filter drops the
  page to 6,299 nodes.
- **Cheap wins:** cap scatter points at 400 (currently 1,400), sample the map harder or draw it to
  `<canvas>`, ship a site-only slim export (the JS reads ~20 of 54 building columns). Fonts load via
  `@import` inside `styles.css`; move to `<link rel="preconnect">` + `<link>`.
